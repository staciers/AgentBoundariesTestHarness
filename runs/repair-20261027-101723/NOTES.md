- **Primitives vs wording** (R3): not distinguishable at this sample size, although on open models

## Exploratory (not pre-registered)

1. **All remaining D2 harm is a key-discipline failure, not a repair failure.**
   - Every one of the 7 harmful D2 episodes (all GLM-5.3 / GLM-5.3-Flash) began with a first
     write that carried **no** idempotency key.
   - With no key, nothing was recorded, so there was nothing to cancel: the 24 failed
     `cancel_partial` calls returned "nothing to cancel". The retry then repeated the committed
     part (2 lasting duplicates, 5 self-corrected).
   - Among episodes whose first write was keyed, D2 harm was 0.
   - The primitive can't repair what was never keyed. That's the same gap the open-confirm study
     found, and the runtime closes it by attaching keys itself.
2. **The baseline arm D was driven by a few model × scenario cells.** Haiku in commerce had 91%
   harm, and GLM models 16–31%. Opus, Sol and Luna had none. That matches the contract study's
   D, where wording that says only "retries are safe" leads some models to patch around a
   partial order rather than replace it.
3. **D1's remaining harm is concentrated in GLM-5.3 in commerce (24%).** Prose guidance leaves the
   choice between completing and cancelling to the agent, and that choice is task-dependent.
   The primitive fixes it.

## What this means for the paper

- **§3 can present execution contracts as providing recovery primitives, not just
  documentation.**
  - A contract that declares an atomic key scope, a structured partial report and a
    `cancel_partial` operation lets every model tested recover from a hidden partial commit as
    safely as if keys weren't offered.
  - That holds provided the agent attaches a key at all.
- **The remaining gap (unkeyed first writes in open models) is exactly what the runtime covers.**
  The allocation is therefore:
  - The **contract** defines the semantics and the repair operations.
  - The **runtime** guarantees keys are used and runs recovery when no agent is involved.
  - An **agent** that does recovery itself is safe only through the contract's primitives.
