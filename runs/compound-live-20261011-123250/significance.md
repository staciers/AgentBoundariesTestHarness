Power planning targets the low end of each difference's 95% CI (Newcombe), not the observed effect, because small-sample effects tend to be inflated. It uses α=0.0125 for confirmatory rows (Bonferroni over 4) and α=0.05 otherwise; rates of 0% or 100% are floored at 2%/98%. `∞` means the CI includes no effect, so no sample size is implied. Extra episodes assume the same arms are extended; a confirmation run should use fresh seeds.
